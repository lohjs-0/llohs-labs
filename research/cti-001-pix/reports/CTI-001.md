
# CTI-001 — The Brazilian Pix Fraud Supply Chain

**Publisher:** LLOHS LABS  
**Classification:** Public Research  
**Status:** Preliminary  
**Research period:** September 2025 – September 2026  
**Data source:** Banco Central do Brasil

---

## 01. Executive Summary

This investigation examines publicly available statistics
associated with suspected fraud and the Brazilian Pix
Special Refund Mechanism (MED).

The research focuses on financial contestations,
refund outcomes, and recorded reasons for
non-recovery of funds.

The analysis covers 13 consecutive monthly records
from September 2025 through September 2026.

### Preliminary observations

- R$ 8.55 billion in accepted contested amounts.
- R$ 1.00 billion in full and partial refunds combined.
- An aggregate calculated refund-to-accepted-amount
  ratio of approximately 11.72%.
- R$ 4.27 billion recorded under non-refund due to
  insufficient balance.

These figures are derived from the collected dataset
and must not be interpreted as the total financial
losses caused by Pix fraud in Brazil.

---

## 02. Research Methodology

### Data acquisition

Data was collected from the Banco Central do Brasil
public OData API.

Resource:

`EstatisticasFraudesPix`

### Processing

The research pipeline includes:

1. API metadata inspection.
2. Public data collection.
3. Monthly record validation.
4. Financial aggregation.
5. Refund and non-refund analysis.
6. Visualization and documentation.

Original records are preserved in the research
repository for reproducibility.

### Data quality

Three monthly records contain small discrepancies
between total contestations and the sum of accepted
and rejected contestations:

| Month | Difference |
|---|---:|
| February 2026 | -1 |
| April 2026 | -4 |
| May 2026 | -2 |

The original values were not modified.

## 03. Findings

### CTI-001-F01
#### Insufficient balance dominates recorded non-refund reasons

**Status:** Preliminary

Across the analyzed period, amounts classified
under insufficient balance totaled approximately
R$ 4.27 billion.

This represents approximately 83% of the combined
amounts recorded in three non-refund categories:

- Insufficient balance
- Closed account
- Other reasons

The finding describes the distribution of reported
non-refund amounts.

It does not establish the movement of funds,
the intent of account holders, or the operational
structure of any criminal organization.

---

## 04. Financial Analysis

The analyzed records contain approximately
R$ 8.55 billion in accepted contested amounts.

Full and partial refunds combined total
approximately R$ 1.00 billion.

The calculated aggregate ratio is 11.72%.

This ratio is a research calculation and should
not automatically be interpreted as an official
consolidated MED recovery rate.

### Non-refund categories

| Category | Recorded amount |
|---|---:|
| Insufficient balance | R$ 4.27 billion |
| Other reasons | R$ 678.08 million |
| Closed account | R$ 198.48 million |

The separate residual non-refunded amount field
totals approximately R$ 2.40 billion.

It is not added to the categories above because
their accounting relationship has not yet been
verified.

---

## 05. Limitations

- MED contestations are not equivalent to all Pix
  fraud incidents.
- Accepted contestations should not be treated as
  a complete measure of criminal activity.
- Public aggregate statistics cannot identify
  individual perpetrators.
- Dataset definitions and revisions require
  continued verification.
- No causal relationship between insufficient
  balance and specific criminal methods has
  been established.
